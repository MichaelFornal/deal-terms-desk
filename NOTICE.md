## Data

**MAUD.** This project uses the Merger Agreement Understanding Dataset (MAUD), by The Atticus Project, licensed
under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Source:
https://huggingface.co/datasets/theatticusproject/maud

MAUD files are downloaded at build time and are not redistributed in this repository. Changes made: the agreements
are normalised, split into passages and indexed, and MAUD's labels serve as the answer key of the human-labelled
evaluation. No label is altered.

**EDGAR.** The technology-target agreements come from the SEC's EDGAR system. The SEC states that its content "is
considered public information and may be copied or further distributed by users of the web site without the SEC's
permission". This repository ships the code that finds and fetches the filings, not the documents or the list of
filings the corpus is built from; the live site quotes clauses and links to each filing.
